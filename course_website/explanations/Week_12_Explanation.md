# Week 12: Model Evaluation & Ensemble Methods

## Overview
Rigorously assessing models and combining their predictive power are essential skills. This week covers advanced performance metrics and techniques to build more stable, powerful models.

## Key Concepts
### 1. Model Evaluation Metrics
- **Beyond Accuracy**: Accuracy is often misleading for imbalanced data. Metrics like precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC/AUC provide a nuanced understanding of a model's performance.

### 2. Ensemble Methods
- **Combining Power**: By combining multiple models (using techniques like voting, bagging, or boosting), we can achieve superior performance and reduce the risk of overfitting, resulting in more stable and robust predictions.
